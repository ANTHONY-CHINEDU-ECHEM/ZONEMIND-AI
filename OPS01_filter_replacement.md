## Purpose
Clogged filters raise fan energy and reduce cooling and heating capacity at the coil.

## Schedule
Inspect pre filters monthly and replace them every three months. Replace bag filters every twelve months or when the pressure drop across them exceeds the manufacturer limit. Record each change in the maintenance log with the date and the pressure reading before and after.

## Notes
Carry out replacements outside occupied hours. The air handling unit must be isolated during the change, so zones will be without ventilation for about thirty minutes.
