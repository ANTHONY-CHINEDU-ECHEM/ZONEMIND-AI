## Purpose
A zone sensor that reads one degree high makes the plant overcool that zone all summer.

## Procedure
Twice a year, compare each zone sensor with a calibrated reference thermometer placed beside it for fifteen minutes. If the difference exceeds 0.3 C, enter an offset at the controller. Replace any sensor that needs an offset of more than 1 C.

## Notes
Check occupancy sensors at the same visit by walking each zone and confirming the status changes at the front end within two minutes.
