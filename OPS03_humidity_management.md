## Purpose
High indoor humidity makes a zone feel warmer than its temperature suggests and encourages mould.

## Guidance
The air handling unit dehumidifies the outdoor air supply to a dew point of 12 C in cooling season. Zone humidity is monitored but not directly controlled. If a zone reads above 65 percent relative humidity for more than four hours, check the outdoor air damper position and the cooling coil condensate drain.

## Notes
Outdoor air free cooling is disabled automatically when the outdoor dew point is above 13 C, to avoid bringing moist air into the building.
