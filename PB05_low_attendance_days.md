## Purpose
Fridays and the days around public holidays see well under half of normal attendance. This note records how the facilities team prepares for them.

## Guidance
Encourage teams to book desks in as few zones as possible so whole zones can stay in set back. Review the booking system the afternoon before and, where a zone has only one or two bookings, contact the people concerned and offer space elsewhere. Do not change zone setpoints by hand; the standby sequence will handle zones that remain booked but empty.

## Rationale
Consolidation is an organisational measure. It makes the automatic strategies more effective but is not itself a control action.
