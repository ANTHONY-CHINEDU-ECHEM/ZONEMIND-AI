## Purpose
This note sets out who is told what, and how quickly, when the control system raises an alarm.

## Levels
Information alarms, such as a setpoint command adjusted by the safety verifier, are logged for weekly review. Warning alarms, such as a zone outside the comfort envelope for more than thirty minutes while occupied, go to the facilities helpdesk. Critical alarms, such as plant failure or a freeze protection trip, page the on call engineer at any hour.

## Response times
Warnings are acknowledged within one working hour. Critical alarms are acknowledged within fifteen minutes.
