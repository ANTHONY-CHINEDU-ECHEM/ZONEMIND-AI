## Purpose
This note describes how the facilities helpdesk logs and responds to hot and cold calls from tenants.

## Procedure
Record the zone, the time and the reported sensation. Check the zone temperature trend for the previous two hours before changing anything. If the zone was inside the comfort envelope, look for local causes such as a blocked diffuser, direct sun on a desk or a draught from a door. Do not adjust zone setpoints by hand for a single complaint; raise a ticket so the pattern can be reviewed.

## Rationale
Manual overrides made in response to one person tend to be forgotten and erode savings for months. A consistent log lets the team see real problems.
