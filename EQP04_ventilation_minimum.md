## Purpose
Occupied zones must receive outdoor air regardless of heating or cooling demand.

## Requirement
While a zone is occupied the air handling unit supplies 10 litres per second per person plus 0.3 litres per second per square metre. Supply fans run at no less than 30 percent flow. A heat recovery wheel preconditions the outdoor air whenever it is colder than 16 C or warmer than 26 C.

## Rationale
Ventilation is a health requirement and is not a lever for energy strategies. Setpoint strategies must never reduce it.
