## Purpose
Twice a year the plant is checked before the main heating and cooling seasons.

## Checklist
Confirm reversing valve operation on every heat pump. Clean outdoor coils. Check refrigerant pressures against commissioning records. Test the heat recovery wheel and its bypass. Verify that holiday dates for the coming season are loaded in the calendar. Review the previous season demand peaks and note the days and times they occurred.

## Notes
The control system selects heating, cooling or shoulder operation each day from the weather forecast. There is no manual seasonal switch to change.
