## Purpose
This document explains the demand component of the electricity bill.

## How it is charged
There are two demand charges. The facilities charge is 5 per kilowatt of the highest quarter hour average load at any time in the month. The peak charge is a further 12 per kilowatt of the highest quarter hour average load inside the peak tariff windows of that month. Each charge is the same whether the peak lasted fifteen minutes or occurred every day.

## What drives it
The facilities charge is usually set in winter during morning warm up on the coldest working day, when every heat pump runs flat out at its lowest efficiency. The peak charge is set on hot summer afternoons. Staggered starts and shallower set back before extreme days defend against the first. Pre cooling followed by a setpoint float inside the window defends against the second, because load moved out of the window no longer counts toward the peak charge.
