## Purpose
On public holidays the building is closed and all zones remain in unoccupied set back unless a booking says otherwise.

## Governance
The facilities manager loads the list of public holidays and planned closure days each December for the year ahead. Changes during the year need written approval from the building manager. Tenants who need access on a closure day must book a zone at least one working day in advance.

## Notes
All hours on a public holiday are charged at the off peak electricity rate, and no critical peak events are called.
