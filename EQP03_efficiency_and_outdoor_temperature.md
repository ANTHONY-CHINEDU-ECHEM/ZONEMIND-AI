## Purpose
This note summarises how plant efficiency varies with the weather, which is the physical reason load shifting works.

## Cooling
The cooling coefficient of performance is about 3.3 at 35 C outdoors and improves by roughly 2.5 percent for every degree the outdoor air is cooler. Cooling delivered in the morning is therefore both cheaper per kilowatt hour and needs fewer kilowatt hours.

## Heating
The heating coefficient of performance is about 3.2 at 7 C outdoors and falls by about 0.045 per degree below that, bottoming out near 1.6 in severe cold. Morning recovery on the coldest days is the least efficient operation of the year.
