## Purpose
A refrigerant leak reduces cooling and heating capacity and is a reportable environmental event.

## Symptoms
A heat pump runs continuously without reaching setpoint, suction pressure is low, or the leak detector in the plant area alarms.

## Procedure
Isolate the affected unit at the front end. Do not reset the unit or attempt to run it. Ventilate the plant area and call the certified refrigeration contractor. Record the estimated charge lost. Neighbouring zones will carry some of the load through the internal partitions until the repair is made.
