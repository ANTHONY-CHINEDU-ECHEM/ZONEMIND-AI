## Purpose
When a float or shed is released, every zone calls for cooling at once and the building can set a new demand peak in the first quarter hour after the window.

## Guidance
Most zones are empty or emptying when the summer window closes at 20:00, so the usual remedy is simply to let them fall into unoccupied set back rather than recover to the normal setpoint. Where zones are still occupied, the rate of change limit already spreads the recovery over more than one decision.

## Rationale
Rebound is a demand charge problem rather than an energy problem. Letting the evening set back absorb it costs nothing.
