## Purpose
A damper stuck open brings in too much outdoor air; one stuck shut starves the building of ventilation and disables free cooling.

## Symptoms
Stuck open: zones struggle to hold setpoint in extreme weather and energy use rises sharply. Stuck shut: night flush has no effect and carbon dioxide readings climb during occupancy.

## Response
Command the damper through its full stroke from the front end and watch the position feedback. If feedback does not follow the command, isolate the actuator and call the maintenance contractor. Do not attempt to compensate by changing zone setpoints.
