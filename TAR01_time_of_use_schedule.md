## Purpose
This document records the energy rates on the supply contract so that operating strategies can be costed.

## Summer schedule
From June to September, working days have a peak window from 15:00 to 20:00 at 0.36 per kilowatt hour, a mid rate from 11:00 to 15:00 and 20:00 to 22:00 at 0.20, and an off peak rate of 0.11 at all other times.

## Winter schedule
From October to May, working days have a peak window from 17:00 to 20:00 at 0.26, a mid rate from 06:00 to 17:00 and 20:00 to 22:00 at 0.15, and an off peak rate of 0.10 at all other times.

## Weekends and holidays
All hours are charged at the off peak rate.
